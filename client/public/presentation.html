1 Executive Summary & Core Value Proposition

SSJ INVOICE™ is an autonomous, multi-tenant Cloud SaaS platform designed to automate end-to-end commercial billing, statutory GST tax compliance, zero-gateway-fee NPCI UPI digital payments, and real-time customer financial ledgers for modern businesses and enterprises.

Key Differentiator: Zero-MDR Dynamic UPI Payment Rails

Traditional ERP platforms require third-party payment gateways (Razorpay, Stripe) deducting 2% to 3% in Merchant Discount Rates (MDR). SSJ INVOICE embeds an autonomous Dynamic NPCI UPI QR Code Engine directly into printed and digital invoices. Customers scan with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM, settling funds directly into the merchant's bank account with ₹0 transaction fees and instantaneous settlement.

4 How Plans Are Assigned

Level 1: SaaS Plans to Business Accounts
  • • Automatic Default on Signup: New businesses start on the Free Plan (10 invoices/mo) with an active 30-day trial automatically.
  • • Direct SuperAdmin 1-Click Dropdown: SuperAdmin logs into /superadmin and selects Free, Pro, or Enterprise directly from the directory table dropdown.
  • • Self-Service Upgrade: Business owners click the Crown icon in the top navigation bar to upgrade to Pro (₹499/mo) or Enterprise (₹4,999/yr).
Level 2: Recurring Retainers to Clients
  • • Client Selection: Open Recurring Invoices and pick a client from your directory.
  • • Billing Frequency: Choose Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly billing.
  • • Itemized Services: Attach catalog services (e.g. ₹5,000 AMC or Retainer).
  • • Auto-Dispatch: The nightly cron daemon automatically generates and issues the invoice on each recurring cycle.

5 Advanced Capabilities & Secret Weapons ("All Other Stuff")

Official Govt Portal GSTR-1 JSON Export

Generates the official schema-compliant JSON file for the GST Portal (gst.gov.in) covering Tables 4, 7, 9B, and 12. Accountants can upload it into the offline tool in seconds.

Public Client Billing & Payment Portal

Every invoice has a shareable web link (/public/invoice/:id). Clients can view, download PDFs, and scan the Dynamic UPI QR code from their mobile browser without logging in.

Dynamic QR Partial Balance Recalculation

When a client pays part of a bill (e.g. ₹5,000 on a ₹15,000 bill), the status becomes PARTIAL and the embedded UPI QR dynamically recalculates to request exactly the remaining ₹10,000.

Input Tax Credit (ITC) Expense Engine

Track operating expenses with vendor GSTINs. The system computes Input Tax Credit (ITC) to offset Output Tax Liability in the GSTR-3B tax matrix, lowering net tax payable.

Statutory Delivery Challan (GST Rule 55)

Legally transport goods for job work, exhibition, trial, or transit before formal invoicing with transporter details and vehicle numbers to prevent vehicle confiscation.

Automated Nightly Cron Auditor (node-cron)

Executes every night at 00:00 to audit tenant subscription expiration dates, lock expired accounts gracefully, reset monthly Free tier quotas, and process recurring invoices.
